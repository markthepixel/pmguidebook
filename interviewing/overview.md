# Overview

## Interviewing: Demonstrating Product Judgment

Product Manager interviews give you an opportunity to explain how you understand problems, make decisions, and work with other people. They also give you a chance to learn whether the role and company are a good fit for you. The skills covered throughout this guide provide a foundation for these discussions, but applying them in an interview requires practice explaining your reasoning with limited time and information.

In this section, we'll explore two aspects of interviewing:

1. [**Preparation**](preparation/): This involves understanding the role and interview process, choosing relevant experience to discuss, and practicing with useful feedback. It also includes evaluating the opportunity and respecting the rules for interview tools and assignments.
2. [**Interview Formats**](interview-formats/): This covers product sense, analytical thinking, behavioral questions, and strategy and technical discussions. Each topic explores how to work through a question, explain your choices, and respond when new information changes your approach.
