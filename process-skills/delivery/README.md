# Delivery

Delivery in product management involves getting a tested product or feature into the hands of its users. This phase involves assessing release readiness and the actual deployment of the product or feature to the end-users. It includes activities such as roll-out and release management, monitoring and analyzing product performance, and gathering user feedback for continuous improvement. The delivery phase requires close collaboration with various teams, including engineering, marketing, sales, and customer support, to ensure a successful product launch and ongoing product success.
